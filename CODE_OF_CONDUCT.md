# Community code of conduct

Be professional, specific, and kind. Disagreement about code, accounting
workflows, security, or product direction is welcome; harassment, discrimination,
threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not.

The unusually important rule in this project is data handling: never post real
client documents, client-identifying filenames, screenshots, or extracted tax
information. Public issue trackers are copied and archived.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary
to protect contributors, users, or client information. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository's security-reporting channel.
