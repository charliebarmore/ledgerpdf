# LedgerPDF support

LedgerPDF is an open-source alpha project. Community reports are welcome, but
Ledger Labs LLC does not promise an individual response time, compatibility
fix, recovery service, or professional support arrangement.

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the public [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/charliebarmore/ledgerpdf/issues)
for ordinary bugs and feature requests. Please:

- reproduce the issue on the newest published release when practical;
- include the LedgerPDF version, operating system, expected behavior, actual
  behavior, and minimal reproduction steps; and
- use synthetic fixtures or a newly created sample document.

**Never upload or paste a real client PDF, binder, source document, filename,
screenshot, page text, tax identifier, credential, or other confidential
information.** Remove identifying metadata from synthetic reproductions too.

## Security reports

Do not report a suspected vulnerability in a public issue. Use GitHub’s private
**Report a vulnerability** form and follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## What support cannot do

LedgerPDF support cannot give accounting, tax, legal, audit, assurance, or
information-security advice. It also cannot retrieve a local binder, restore a
deleted file, or reconstruct an engagement. Ledger Labs does not operate a cloud
copy of your files. Maintain independent backups and follow your firm’s recovery
procedures.

General non-confidential questions may be sent to info@ledgerlabs.co.
